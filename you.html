Why it works

Picture the steps, not the result

In a UCLA study, students who pictured themselves studying studied more, scored higher and felt less anxious than students who pictured the grade.

Pham & Taylor, 1999

Specific plans get done

Across 94 studies, deciding exactly when and how you'll act made people much more likely to reach their goals.

Gollwitzer & Sheeran, 2006

Give it two months

New daily habits took about 66 days to stick on average, and missing a day didn't undo the progress.

Lally et al., UCL, 2009

Make your film

Each morning it trains your mind to see you as someone who does these things. Pick what matters, add your people, check your scenes. The preview updates as you go.

Your goals

This year or ten years from now. Tick what you want in your life, and a couple of quick questions turn each one into the steps you take today.

Make each goal yours

Write the things you actually do, in your own words, and they go in your film just as you wrote them. Leave any blank and we'll fill it in for you.

Your scenes

These are the scenes in your film, built from what you picked. Keep them, tweak the words, or change the picture. Each theme ends with where it leads, however long that takes.

Pick your song

Three ways to add music: a classic or a track made for your film below, an MP3 of your own, or save it without music and add any song inside Instagram or TikTok.

Classics

Famous classical recordings in the public domain. Free to use anywhere, including when you share your film.

Made for your film

Original music composed to fit your film exactly, scene changes on the beat. Free to use anywhere, including when you share it.

Or your own song

Buying a song on Bandcamp gets you the MP3 and pays the artist directly. Songs you add are for your own viewing; posting a film with someone else's song publicly can break copyright.

Bring your film across

iPhone keeps home screen apps separate from your browser, so your film needs one tap to come over.

  1. Open motionmornings.com in the browser where you made your film, Safari or Chrome.

  3. Come back here and tap Paste my film.

Privacy

Your film stays with you.

Everything you put into Motion Mornings stays on your own phone or computer: your name, your photos, your goals, the songs you add and the videos you save. None of it is uploaded to us or anyone else, and we can't see it.
