KIDS
For the big moments

Show them what's going to happen, before it happens.

A short cartoon film of your child doing the new thing, step by step, in your voice. Playgroup, trying a new food, a busy room. Watch it together until it feels familiar.

Who's in it

Your child and the grown-up going with them. Add someone they'll meet, a brother or sister, or a baby if the story needs them. Tap a picture to change how they look.

The story

Write what will happen, in order, the way you'd explain it to them. Each step is one picture. Keep it calm and true to the day.

Your voice

Read each line, one at a time, the way you'd read a bedtime story. Each picture stays on screen for as long as you talk. You can skip this and the words still show on screen.

Music

Soft music plays underneath and drops down while you're talking.

Watch and save

Play it with the sound on. If anything's not quite right, go back and change it.

Save it as a video

Save it to your phone so you can watch it anywhere, any time. It plays through once while it saves, so keep this screen open until it's done.
