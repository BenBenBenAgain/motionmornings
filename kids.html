Make your film

Add your child and their people, pick a story or write your own, then read it aloud. The preview updates as you go.

Who's in it

Your child and the grown-up going with them. Add someone they'll meet, a brother or sister, or a baby if the story needs them. Tap a picture to change how they look.

The story

Write what will happen, in order, the way you'd explain it to them. Each step is one picture. Keep it calm and true to the day. Up to 10 steps, and the film can be up to a minute and a half.

Your voice

Read each line, one at a time, the way you'd read a bedtime story. Each line can be up to 8 seconds. Each picture stays on screen for as long as you talk. You can skip this and the words still show on screen.

Music

Soft music plays underneath and drops down while you're talking.

Watch and save

Play it with the sound on. If anything's not quite right, go back and change it.

Save it as a video

Save it to your phone so you can watch it anywhere, any time. It plays through once while it saves, so keep this screen open until it's done.

Request a new picture

Each new picture is created individually, so this isn’t instant. We’ll email you when it’s ready, usually within a few days, and it’ll then be in the picture list for your story. In the meantime, use the closest picture, or tick “Show as big calm words” to show just the words.
